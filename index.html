STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: LAB EQUIPMENT CONTINGENCY & INVENTORY MANUAL

Document Code: KSAU-HS/CAMS-RT/SOP-01 • Effective Scope: Labs CM-01-158 & CM-01-162

1 Purpose & Laboratory Scope

This instruction manual establishes the standardized protocol for tracking medical equipment readiness, reporting sudden device faults, allocating backup units during clinical simulation sessions, and maintaining an accurate inventory across Respiratory Therapy Skills Labs CM-01-158 and CM-01-162.

2 Equipment Status Definitions

  • • Operational: Fully functional and ready for student clinical training.
  • • Contingency / Fault: Device experienced an operational fault; backup unit allocated.
  • • Not Available: Temporarily out of service or undergoing external repair.
  • *Note: Routine calibration & preventive maintenance (PM) are managed directly by HTMS.

3 Step-by-Step Procedure When a Device Faults

  1. Access Portal: Scan the Lab Door QR Code or open the web portal and sign in.
  2. Locate Unit: Filter by Lab (CM-01-158 or CM-01-162) or search by HTMS # / KU #.
  3. Update Status: Select Contingency / Fault or Not Available from the row dropdown (or click Log Issue).
  4. Document Reason & Backup: Enter the exact Fault Reason (Observation) and specify the Backup HTMS Unit Assigned so clinical training continues uninterrupted.

4 Restoring Status & Auto-Backup Protocol

  1. When a faulty or unavailable unit is repaired and verified ready for lab use, click Reset Status on the device row (or Reset All Statuses to Operational).
  2. Automatic Data Protection: The portal automatically downloads a timestamped multi-sheet Excel (.xlsx) archive containing all pre-reset fault reasons before clearing the active Contingency Logbook.

5 Adding New Units or Removing Obsolete Equipment

• New Arrivals: Click + Add Medical Equipment to register new systems and attach sub-components (Simpads, racks, modules).
• Obsolete Units: If an item is permanently decommissioned or deemed obsolete, click the red Trash (Remove Obsolete) icon on its row (or inside Edit) and confirm permanent removal.
